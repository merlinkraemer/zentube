## Long description

YouTube is great. YouTube's interface is not.

ZenTube strips away everything designed to pull your attention away from what you actually came to watch:

- **Sidebar and guide** — gone
- **Notification bell** — gone
- **Filter chips** (Trending, Gaming, Mixes…) — gone
- **"Come back to Premium" banners** — gone
- **Related topic chips** on watch pages — gone
- **Voice search button** — gone
- **Create button** — gone

What's left is the video, the search bar, and you.

One toggle in the popup to turn it on or off. No settings, no accounts, no tracking. Just a cleaner YouTube.

Works on all YouTube pages. Toggle syncs across tabs instantly.
